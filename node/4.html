Rates & Insurance

Rates

$140-$190 per 45-50 minute session

Insurance

Services are usually covered in full or in part by your health insurance or employee benefit plan. I am an out-of-network provider with most insurers and will provide you with a fully coded receipt to submit to your insurance company.

Please check your coverage carefully by asking the following questions:

  • Do I have mental health insurance benefits?
  • What is my deductible and has it been met?
  • How many sessions per year does my health insurance cover?
  • What is the coverage amount per therapy session?

Reduced Fee

Reduced fee services are available on a limited basis.

Payment

Cash, check and all major credit cards accepted for payment.

Cancellation Policy

If you do not show up for your scheduled therapy appointment, and you have not notified us at least 24 hours in advance, you will be required to pay the full cost of the session.
